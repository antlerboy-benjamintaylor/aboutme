How this works

The canonical object is the idea, not a particular PDF filename. The machine-readable source is manifest.json. It records publication status, formats, known short routes and a first-pass rights judgement.

Some older folders contain third-party material. They are marked for review and aren't treated as blanket permission to republish. The catalogue will grow as old social posts, SharePoint external shares and short-link histories are reconciled.
