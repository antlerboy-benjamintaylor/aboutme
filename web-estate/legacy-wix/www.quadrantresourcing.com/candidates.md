What we are looking for…

Our candidates join a growing team who are passionate about adding value to people and places. You must want to work with our clients to achieve their outcomes, in a way that works for you both.

​

You are:

passionate

authentic, approachable and confidential

respectful and sensitive to people and the way they want you to work

able to demonstrate and evidence a clear and tangible track record of success

Quadrant Resourcing is the specialist recruitment arm of RedQuadrant, one of the UK’s leading independent management consultancies. We work closely with our clients on a day to day basis, whether we provide consultancy, learning and development, or interim management support. So you are not just placed in an interim position, you have the security of a resource bank of people to ask for help and advice, and a support network.

​

We care about you and your development, and whether you want to stretch yourself. We work hard to match you to a role and organisation where you can shine and add value to your skills. We have built many long term, ongoing partnerships, providing us with a deep understanding of our clients' history, culture, and values. This is part of getting you the right interim match.

​

We provide support on wide-ranging, interesting, and exciting pieces of work. As the resourcing arm of RedQuadrant, we offer appropriate consultancy opportunities on a part-time or full-time basis, along with access to a number of consultancy frameworks, much of which the traditional agency model is unable to provide. So we will seek to match you for both if that works for you.
