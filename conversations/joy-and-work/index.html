Conversations with Benjamin P Taylor

Joy and work

Conversations about doing useful work, becoming ourselves, and making organisations more human.

Why joy and work?

Work occupies a great deal of our lives. It can give us purpose, companionship, and the satisfaction of doing something well. It can also make those things unnecessarily difficult. This series asks what people discover when they take both the work and the person doing it seriously.

The questions

  • When does work feel worthwhile?
  • What helps people bring judgement, care, and imagination to it?
  • How do authority, routines, and expectations shape the experience?
  • What can we change together, and what starts with ourselves?
