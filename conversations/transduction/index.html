Conversations with Benjamin P Taylor

Transduction

Conversations about systems, cybernetics, complexity, and the work of changing things.

What this series follows

Systems ideas become interesting when they change what we notice, how we act, and what we take responsibility for. These conversations follow that movement between ideas and experience.

Questions we keep returning to

  • What does a situation look like from the different positions within it?
  • Which habits, measures, and arrangements keep bringing the same difficulties back?
  • What does systems practice make possible in the work itself?
  • How do we learn whether an intervention is helping?
